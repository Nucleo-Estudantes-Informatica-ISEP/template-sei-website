# NN — Short title of the decision

## Context

The problem being solved and the constraints in play. What forced a choice
to be made — a real alternative existed and was passed over, not just "this
is how it was implemented."

## Discussion

The options considered, each with enough detail to see why it was a real
candidate:

- **A. \<option\>** — \<what it involves\>
- **B. \<option\>** — \<what it involves\>
- **C. \<option\>** — \<what it involves\>

Reference the actual issue/PR/discussion where this was worked out, when one
exists.

## Decision

Which option was chosen, and why — the reasoning that made it win over the
others listed above.

### Consequences

What this commits to, what it rules out, and what it costs. Include the
tradeoff, not just the upside — a decision with no downside usually isn't
one that needed recording.
